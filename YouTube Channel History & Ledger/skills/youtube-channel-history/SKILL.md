# YouTube Channel Metadata & History with Antigravity

This skill enables autonomous agents to maintain, query, and synchronize long-term YouTube channel memory, complete video metadata ledgers, prompt evolution archives, and publishing cadence analytics.

## When to Use This Skill

Activate this skill when:
- Querying past videos, historical prompts, titles, descriptions, or tags.
- Verifying character continuity ("MARROW" ivory skeleton, cyan silhouette, amber eyes, Roman wool tunic).
- Recording a newly rendered, merged, or queued video into the permanent channel chronicle.
- Checking channel publishing cadence, turnaround velocity, or next scheduled upload slot.
- Updating channel memory (lore, audience personas, production rules, milestones).
- Synchronizing NexusFlow (`Prompt.txt`, `Output`, `Result`) and YouTube Uploader queue.

## Production Directives & Continuity Locks

1. **Protagonist "MARROW"**:
   - Always an ivory-white anatomical skeleton.
   - Faintly translucent cyan glass silhouette.
   - Glowing amber eye lights inside eye sockets.
   - Plain undyed wool Roman tunic with leather belt and coin pouch. Strictly no modern clothing.
2. **Video Format**:
   - YouTube Shorts: Vertical 9:16, 24 fps, 24.0s duration (3 scenes x 8s).
   - Hook in first 2 seconds; cliffhanger in final 2 seconds.
3. **Pillars**:
   - Ancient Roman/Greek hydraulic mechanisms, subterranean cisterns, lost imperial tombs, comet impact ruins.
