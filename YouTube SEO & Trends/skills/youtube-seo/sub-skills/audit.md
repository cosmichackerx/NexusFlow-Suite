# Algorithmic SEO Audit Sub-Skill

This sub-skill provides a quantitative 0-100 quality scorecard evaluating metadata packages against YouTube's 2026 ranking algorithm.

## The 6 Scoring Pillars

| Pillar | Max Points | Core Factors Checked |
|---|---|---|
| **Title Optimization** | 20 pts | Length <= 100, mobile truncation safety (40-70 chars), curiosity gap, `#Shorts` tag if vertical. |
| **Description Architecture** | 20 pts | Rich context (300+ chars), keywords above "Show More" fold, timestamp pacing, explicit CTA. |
| **Tag Saturation & Quality** | 15 pts | Allocation between 250-485 chars, 8-25 tag count, presence of brand and long-tail tags. |
| **Hashtag Strategy** | 15 pts | Exactly 3 primary hashtags, `#Shorts` included for vertical, topical relevance. |
| **Engagement Velocity** | 15 pts | Pinned comment trigger question, duration optimization for high retention (>90%). |
| **SERP & AI Crawlability** | 15 pts | Schema.org VideoObject JSON-LD presence, HD resolution verification. |

## Grading Scale

- **95 - 100**: `A+ (Elite Algorithmic Readiness)` - Top 1% of creators on the platform.
- **85 - 94**: `A (High-Performing)` - Optimized to outrank competing content.
- **75 - 84**: `B (Good)` - Solid foundation with minor opportunities.
- **< 75**: `C (Needs Revision)` - Missing key ranking signals.
