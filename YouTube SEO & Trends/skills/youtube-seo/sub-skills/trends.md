# Viral Trend Hunter Sub-Skill

This sub-skill uncovers high-demand, high-RPM, trending topics specifically engineered to elevate the channel into the top tier of YouTube's algorithm.

## Virality Scoring Model

Each trend is scored on:
- **Search Volume Tier**: High monthly interest (50K - 500K searches/month)
- **Competition Density**: Preference for underserved angles with low or medium creator saturation
- **Curiosity Gap Potential**: Capacity to form a compelling 2-second hook that halts scrolling
- **RPM Potential**: Advertiser appeal in history, education, documentaries, and tech ($10 - $30 RPM)
- **Continuity Fit**: Synergy with our protagonist mascot `Marrow` and previous episodic storylines.
