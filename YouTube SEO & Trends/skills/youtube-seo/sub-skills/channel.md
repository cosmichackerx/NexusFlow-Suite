# Channel Management & Growth Strategy Sub-Skill

This sub-skill manages the YouTube channel identity, Studio authentication status, brand positioning, and algorithmic authority building.

## Channel Identity: Bones of Empires

- **Channel Name**: `Bones of Empires`
- **Handle**: `@BonesOfEmpires`
- **Channel Mascot**: `Marrow` (An articulate ivory skeleton in Roman wool tunic with glowing amber eye-lights)
- **Primary Niche**: Ancient History, Archaeological Mysteries, Lost Civilizations
- **Aesthetic**: Hyper-detailed 3D CGI realism (Pixar meets BBC documentary), teal & amber grade, 24fps

## Key Operational Workflows

1. **Verify Session Health**:
   - Check if Playwright profile in `%APPDATA%\nexusflow-youtube-uploader\playwright-youtube-profile` is authenticated.
   - Run `node cli.js status` or call `youtube_seo_status`.
   - If session expires, launch YouTube Uploader to re-authenticate with Google.

2. **Niche Authority Checklist**:
   - Maintain strict narrative and visual continuity across all uploads.
   - Use identical protagonist mascot (`Marrow`) to anchor repeat viewership and algorithmic recognition.
   - Position content at the intersection of proven historical intrigue and cutting-edge 3D CGI.

3. **Competitor Benchmarking**:
   - *Voices of the Past* -> Focus on source realism; we add cinematic visual immersion.
   - *History Bypass* -> Focus on fast viral pacing; we add narrative world-building and higher visual fidelity.
   - *Fall of Civilizations* -> Focus on epic solemn atmosphere; we condense into high-velocity 24-60s Shorts.
