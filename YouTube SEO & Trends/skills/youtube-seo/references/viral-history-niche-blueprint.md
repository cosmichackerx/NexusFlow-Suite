# Viral History Niche Blueprint: Bones of Empires

Channel Strategy & Production Playbook for Dominating the Ancient History, Archaeology, and 3D CGI Mystery Space on YouTube.

## The Bones of Empires Formula

1. **Character-Driven Archeology (`Marrow`)**:
   - Rather than dry voiceover over stock footage, viewers explore ancient ruins alongside a recurring protagonist mascot: `Marrow`, a polished ivory skeleton with glowing amber eyes and a translucent cyan silhouette.
   - This creates parasocial attachment, fan art, and instant thumb-stopping visual branding.

2. **Visual Differentiation**:
   - High-contrast volumetric teal and amber color grade.
   - Cinematic 3D CGI with realistic physical weight, water reflections, mist, and macro textures.
   - 24 fps cinematic cadence rather than flat 60 fps gaming visuals.

3. **Multi-Part Episodic Continuity**:
   - Each Short ends on a revelation or threshold crossing (e.g. stepping through a portal of starlight).
   - This drives viewers to click through to the channel profile, subscribe, and binge previous episodes.

4. **Monetization & RPM Optimization**:
   - Ancient history, archaeology, and educational science attract high-income, curiosity-driven demographics.
   - RPM ranges from **$10 to $25+**, significantly outperforming gaming or general entertainment ($2 - $4).
