# High-CTR Title Frameworks

Psychological patterns proven across millions of video impressions to drive maximum Click-Through Rate.

## The 5 Core Frameworks

### 1. The Unresolved Curiosity Gap
- *Structure*: `[Subject] Found [Vague Object/Anomaly] Under [Familiar Location]... 💀`
- *Example*: `He Found Something Under Alexandria... 💀 #Shorts`
- *Psychology*: The brain experiences cognitive itch; it cannot resolve the missing information without watching the video.

### 2. The Forbidden Knowledge Angle
- *Structure*: `The [Time Period] [Object/Secret] That Was Never Supposed to Be Found`
- *Example*: `The 2,000-Year-Old Mechanism Buried Under Ancient Alexandria`
- *Psychology*: Implies suppression, conspiracy, or buried truth.

### 3. The Bracketed Context Lift (+20-30% CTR)
- *Structure*: `[Curiosity Hook]... ([Exact Search Entity])`
- *Example*: `Archaeologists Found THIS Under Alexandria... (Archimedes Mechanism)`
- *Psychology*: The bracket provides instant reassurance of the subject matter while keeping the emotional hook intact.

### 4. The Urgent Anomaly
- *Structure*: `It Wasn't Buried to Be Forgotten... ⚙️`
- *Psychology*: Flips common assumptions on their head.

### 5. Mobile Truncation Rules
- Mobile vertical feeds truncate titles past **45-50 characters**.
- Always ensure the emotional trigger and the primary keyword appear within the first **35 characters**.
